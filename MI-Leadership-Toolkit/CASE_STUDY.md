# Case Study: Making Parcel Performance Clearer for EVRI

> **Interview scenario:** This shows how I would apply the framework in an EVRI setting. It is not a claim that I delivered a project or measured results at EVRI.

In a parcel business, managers need three answers: are parcels on time, where are delays happening, and which depots need support? Different measures or missing scans make reports hard to trust.

I tackle this in five steps: **listen, agree, check, show and measure.**

1. **Listen – Stakeholder Operations:** I ask depot and network teams what decisions they face and what reports they use now.
2. **Agree – Strategic Data Governance:** I agree shared definitions for on-time delivery, first-time success and delivery exceptions.
3. **Check – Data Quality Delivery:** I check scans are complete and up to date, and depot and route details are correct. I log issues and help find their cause.
4. **Show – Practical Data Templates:** I use standard SQL and dashboards to show network trends and help depots spot local delays.
5. **Measure – Business Value & ROI:** I track report use, actions taken and changes in delivery performance over time.

This connects trusted information to practical decisions. I only claim improvement when I can show a verified starting point, timeframe and evidence.
